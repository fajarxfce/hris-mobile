# Bounded global history recovery completion

A selected account metadata row can advance after a finite local history visit, including empty
internal metadata and independently confirmed source/metadata unavailability. The completion owner
rechecks account, native session, current Space root, installed source and exact registered device,
actual Company/Space locator and historical traversal device, immutable metadata and bounded cursor
coverage. Published Original aliases keep their logical Space coordinates. Cross-company/Space
source absence is explicit waiting; malformed source, permission denial and storage failure do not
become successful unavailability.

Pending input lists are copied before suspension and capped at16. Existing traversal progress is
inspected twice without requesting a generation; admitted local page receipts establish finite
progress. Same-generation EOF survives local promotion, while another pass cannot finish the global
generation. A completed unresolved pass sleeps until new actual demand. An absent progress read is
reconfirmed before completion and cannot hide newly published progress.

One exact demand CAS advances only the selected current recovery revision, retaining independent
primary/source/inventory work and a concurrent requested generation. The existing incoming wake
owner remains unchanged. Returned snapshots are copied before suspension. Both a valid changed
write receipt and actual retained advancement are required; an uncertain storage reply may be
confirmed by the exact next turn. Null, fabricated, unchanged or further-advanced evidence does not
manufacture a receipt. No automatic write retry occurs.

The separate deferral owner accepts operational failures and stores the established bounded retry
schedule without advancing metadata or inspecting content. Account/source/root/registration changes,
authority failures, invalid state and queue contention are not converted to operational retry.
Repeated old captures do not increment retry again. Both local operations have five-second deadlines
and propagate external cancellation. Their Koin factories are explicitly resolved by graph tests.

Selected-page equality is not a whole-account snapshot: locator storage has no inventory version.
The serialized worker must preserve metadata-before-request causality, including restore and source
changes. Worker history selection/adoption/service, new-key producers, callback ownership and
end-to-end fairness remain unactivated and require their own validation. No native SDK execution,
phone installation, live deployment or Starter policy port is claimed by these domain checks.
