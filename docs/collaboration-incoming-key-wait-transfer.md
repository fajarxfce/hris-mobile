# Original transfer from primary work to key recovery

`DemoteCollaborationSpaceOriginalIncomingRoom` moves a still-current pending Original
task with unresolved Company content into a fresh history-recovery request. It validates
current owned account/native session, immutable Company metadata, installed source and
registered original physical device around suspended reads and writes. The historical
Company traversal remains separate from the Group device receipt.

A one-reference local read proves provider EOF, a finished finite pending seek and an
unresolved retained event. A requested head must have an exact retained source and matching
checkpoint; either resolved or unresolved head content proves provider coverage. Missing
heads, unfinished provider work and changed final progress keep primary work. Clean content
continues through its existing completion owner. No transport, key or content acquisition,
reference resolution, acknowledgement or global completion occurs in this operation.

Only after these checks is a fresh recovery identity allocated. One existing demand CAS
stores exact primary removal, the new/coalesced history request and a fresh incoming wake.
Concurrent primary/source work survives. An absent or replaced capture creates no extra
request, including replay after an uncertain successful commit. Successful repository
snapshots are copied before suspension. A unique retained request can confirm a lost
storage response. Null, malformed, fabricated and unconfirmed replies cannot be receipts.
Admission change, timeout and cancellation prevent success. There is one five-second local
invocation and no internal write retry. The data module owns constructor composition; the
existing graph test explicitly resolves the new factory. The worker does not invoke it yet.

## Finite visit and replacement policies

`planCollaborationHistoryRecoveryVisit` inspects a local read, its successful bounded page
receipt if work occurred, and current checkpoint facts without requesting another opaque
generation. It checks stable history/provider identity, monotonic revision/reference counts,
receipt generation/cursor and all outcome counts. Company history keeps its absent provider;
Space history keeps its explicit provider. An exact same-generation EOF receipt survives
promotion of a later pass. Finishing an older different pass cannot complete the requested
pass. Unresolved content alone does not cause endless metadata rounds.

`planCollaborationSpaceIncomingKeyDemotion` plans primary removal and a fresh recovery
request together. It preserves unrelated primary rooms, source/inventory/physical lanes and
an active recovery cursor/retry. Absent/replaced tasks are no-ops. Changed facts under one
task identity and reused active/requested recovery IDs fail. Original and published alias
targets keep distinct identities, including a full64-room queue. Its separate confirmation
policy requires the retained unique request and absence/replacement of the original task.
These pure policies do not provide native authority or manufacture durable receipts.

## Coverage and remaining integration

The traversal codec accepts only published checkpoints. Reading actual unresolved progress
therefore establishes metadata publication before primary retirement requests a later pass.
This is causal ordering, not a transaction spanning history and incoming-demand stores.
The incoming worker must serialize provider writes and transfer, as for existing primary
completion. The demand CAS fences competing producer updates.

Account locator pages have no account-wide revision. Equal page rereads cannot exclude an
insertion behind the scan cursor. Equivalent Space transfer and source/backup restoration
must request recovery after metadata publication too, before global completion is enabled.
Test key-before-metadata, insertions before/after EOF, concurrent resolution and lost replies
across those producers. Normal key callbacks should target known rooms; account scans remain
fallback for loss/overflow/unknown mapping or source/backup restoration. Confirmed global
completion, operational deferral, worker service, callback occurrence retention and native
listener drain remain required. No producer is activated by this change.
